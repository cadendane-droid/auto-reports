Strongest signal

Even at near-zero volume, the capture→extract→query loop still completes correctly when it's used: this week's one source_submitted event converted to processing_completed (1 of 1), and of the 3 query_asked events this week, 1 went beyond turn 1 to turn 2 — a genuine multi-turn interaction, not just a single drive-by query. Quality of engagement is holding up even though reach/volume has dropped sharply.

Biggest concern

Activity collapsed broadly and almost in sync this week: new signups 1 → 0, source_submitted -94% (18 → 1), active users -83% (6 → 1), query_asked -50% (6 → 3), and supporting feature events graph_opened -76% (21 → 5) and profile_viewed -85% (33 → 5) all dropped together. At ~10 pilot users a quiet week is plausible, but the breadth and synchrony of the drop across otherwise-unrelated event types is also consistent with a tracking or deployment regression — that possibility hasn't been ruled out.

  1. 1. Validate the extraction pipeline against a real sample before trusting this week's 100% success rate. The last statistically meaningful read (last week, n=18 source_submitted) converted only 15 of 18 to processing_completed — 83.3%, below the ~95% operational threshold flagged in prior reports. This week's "100%" is a single data point (n=1) and does not confirm that issue was fixed. Recommend pulling extraction job logs (e.g. the Inngest extract job) for last week's 3 failed runs and re-checking success rate again once source_submitted volume returns to double digits.
  2. 2. Investigate whether this week's near-total activity drop (signups 1→0, source_submitted -94%, active users -83%, plus parallel declines in graph_opened and profile_viewed) reflects real user pause or a tracking/deployment break. The synchrony across unrelated event types is the key signal here — a behavioral lull would more likely hit some events harder than others. Recommend a direct check with the ~10 pilot users on usage status, alongside a quick verification that the SDK/tracking snippet is still firing correctly in production after any recent deploy.
