Strongest signal

The capture→extract→query loop fully recovered this week. Last week, all 3 people who submitted a source got it extracted successfully, but none of them went on to ask a query — a complete drop-off at the last step (100% → 100% → 0%). This week, all 4 people who submitted a source completed extraction and asked a query (100% → 100% → 100%), with extraction success bouncing back to 100% (from 58%) and query volume going from 0 to 9.

Biggest concern

Total activity volume fell sharply even as active users held steady: source_submitted is down 74% (19 → 5) and person_added is down 72% (18 → 5) week-over-week. Separately and more structurally, account_created has fired only once in the product's entire 60-day history against an intended cohort of ~10 real users — top-of-funnel signup tracking or acquisition, not in-product conversion, looks like the binding constraint.

  1. 1. Investigate the capture-volume contraction (source_submitted: 19 → 5, -74%; person_added: 18 → 5, -72%) even though extraction and query conversion are both fully healthy this week. Neither of the two usual trigger conditions applies right now — extraction success is at 100% (well above the 95% operational threshold) and there's no drop-off at the query step — so this isn't a pipeline or motivation problem, it's a volume problem upstream of capture. Check whether last week's 19 captures were a one-off burst (e.g., one user's bulk session) that simply hasn't repeated, versus a genuine slowdown in steady-state usage.
  2. 2. Audit signup tracking and top-of-funnel acquisition. account_created has fired exactly once across the full 60-day history, even though 3-4 distinct people are actively submitting sources and asking queries most weeks. Either most real users of the product never fire a tracked signup event (an instrumentation gap worth fixing) or only one person in the ~10-person rollout cohort has formally been onboarded so far (an acquisition gap worth a direct check-in) — worth confirming which before reading anything further into the activation-rate KPI above.
