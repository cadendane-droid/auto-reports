Strongest signal

Existing-user engagement grew even with zero new top-of-funnel activity: real-action WAU rose from 1 to 3 and query_asked volume rose from 3 to 14 week-over-week (+367%), and the query traffic now comes from 3 distinct users rather than one repeat user. The product is pulling people back in and earning more use per user; the shortage is upstream, in top-of-funnel volume, not in-app engagement.

Biggest concern

Two things, not one. First, new-user activity is at zero for a third consecutive week: 0 signups, 0 captures, 0 new people, 0 extractions this week and last week (the last real capture activity was the week of Jun 22). Second, and more structural: over the trailing 4 weeks the extraction pipeline only succeeded on 63% of submissions (17 of 27) — real failures in the weeks of Jun 8 (33%) and Jun 15 (58%). That figure hasn't moved because no new submissions have occurred in over 2 weeks to test whether it's fixed, meaning the pipeline's actual current reliability is still unverified.

  1. 1. Over the trailing 4 weeks, only 63% of submissions completed extraction (17 of 27: 33% the week of Jun 8, 58% the week of Jun 15, then 100% the one week with a submission since). That's the strongest-evidence signal in this report — it's backed by real volume, unlike the single-user funnel. Recommend inspecting the Inngest extract job and re-running the pipeline verification script before investing further in activation work, since a historically ~37% silent-failure rate on source_submitted → processing_completed would invalidate any funnel or query-depth conclusion drawn from that period, and there's been no new submission volume in 2+ weeks to confirm it's since been fixed.
  2. 2. account_created has fired exactly once in Almura's tracked history (Jun 23) and zero times in each of the last three 7-day windows. Meanwhile the one cohort that did sign up converted 100% end-to-end, and existing-user engagement is climbing (WAU +200%, queries +367% week-over-week) — the in-app funnel is not the bottleneck. Recommend focusing on top-of-funnel acquisition (direct outreach/invites to the pilot list) rather than in-app activation nudges, since there is currently no new-signup flow to optimize.
