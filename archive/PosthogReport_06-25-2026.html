Strongest signal

Extraction fully recovered this week: 7 of 7 source_submitted (100%) reached processing_completed, up from 8 of 17 (47.1%) last week. Every capture that came in got processed cleanly through to a query-ready state.

Biggest concern

Captures fell 59% week-over-week (17 → 7), with new-person additions dropping in lockstep (16 → 7). Active users and queries both rose, so this isn't a broad engagement collapse — it's concentrated specifically in the act of submitting new sources.

  1. 1. Treat the extraction-rate recovery as encouraging, not confirmed. 100% (7/7) follows 47.1% (8/17) the week before — at single-digit weekly volumes, one bad batch can swing the rate by dozens of points. Watch the next 1–2 weeks before concluding the pipeline issue is resolved; if extraction success rate dips back below ~70%, prioritize an operational fix on the source_submitted → processing_completed step.
  2. 2. Investigate the capture-volume drop (source_submitted: 17 → 7, -59%) before it compounds. Active users rose +50% (4 → 6) and queries rose +20% (5 → 6) in the same week, so users who are active are engaging more, not less — but fewer of them (or fewer of the same users) are submitting new sources. Check whether this traces to existing users capturing less, or to a stall in bringing in new contributors.
