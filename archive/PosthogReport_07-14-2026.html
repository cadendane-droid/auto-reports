What This Means

Extraction is healthy — 100% success rate for three straight weekly buckets — so the pipeline is not the bottleneck right now. The bottleneck is earlier: only half of this week's two signups ever captured a person in the first place. Everyone who did capture went on to a successful extraction and a query with zero further drop-off, which means fixing the very first step would likely flow straight through to a query.

Query depth and capture-speed remain unreadable this week: turn_index is unset on all 17 query_asked events (despite carrying real values earlier in the project's history), and ms_to_node_visible is missing on all 9 person_capture_timing events despite the events themselves firing. Both look like standing instrumentation gaps rather than one-off blips — worth a quick engineering check independent of the growth work below.
