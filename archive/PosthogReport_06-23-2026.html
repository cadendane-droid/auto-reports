Strongest signal

Despite zero new signups, the existing users went much deeper into the core workflow: contacts added grew from 6 to 15 (+150%) and notes submitted grew from 8 to 14 (+75%). Backend processing kept pace too — completions jumped from 1 to 11, well ahead of last week's near-stalled pipeline.

Biggest concern

AI queries run dropped from 7 to 0 this week, weekly active users fell from 4 to 1, and no new users joined — the rollout has stalled exactly at the step that proves value. The lifetime funnel confirms it structurally: 100% of users add a contact and a note, but only 25% (1 of 4) ever run an AI query.

  1. 1. Close the gap between "Note Captured" and "First AI Query." The onboarding funnel shows 4/4 users add a contact and submit a note (100% each step), but only 1/4 (25%) ever runs an AI query — and query_asked volume fell from 7 last week to 0 this week even as person_added (+150%) and source_submitted (+75%) both grew. Trigger an in-app suggested query (e.g., "Ask Almura: what do I know about this person?") immediately after processing_completed fires, so the first AI query happens inside the same session instead of being a separate, skippable step.
  2. 2. Re-engage the cohort and resume signups — both stalled this week. WAU fell from 4 to 1 (-75%) and new signups went from 4 to 0 (-100%), with only 4 of the ~10-user target cohort onboarded so far. Reach out directly (email/Slack) to the 3 users who were active last week but not this week to find the blocker, and resume outreach to bring in the next pilot users — at n=4, losing even one user swings every metric by 25%.
