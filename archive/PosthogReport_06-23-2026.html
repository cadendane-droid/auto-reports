Strongest signal

Capture activity is up sharply: source_submitted grew from 8 → 22 (+175%) and person_added from 6 → 21 (+250%) week over week. The people actually using Almura are putting meaningfully more into it.

Biggest concern

Extraction is still failing on a large share of submissions: only 14 of 22 source_submitted (63.6%) completed processing this week, up from just 1 of 8 (12.5%) last week — improving, but both far below the ~95% bar that would indicate a healthy pipeline. With n=1 signup this week, the activation funnel itself has nothing meaningful to say; the extraction success rate is the real story.

  1. 1. Treat the extraction pipeline as broken until proven otherwise. Extraction success rate (processing_completed / source_submitted) is 63.6% this week and 12.5% last week — both well under the ~95% threshold that would signal a healthy "silent-failure-free" pipeline. A broken pipeline invalidates every downstream number in this report (queries, activation, capture speed). Before any product or onboarding work, inspect the Inngest extract job logs for this week's failed submissions and re-run the pipeline verification script to find why over a third of this week's submissions — and seven-eighths of last week's — never reached processing_completed.
  2. 2. Add an automated alert on the source_submitted → processing_completed ratio. This failure mode is currently only visible a week later in a report like this one. A simple threshold alert (e.g., notify if the rolling 24h ratio drops under ~90%) would catch the next silent-failure episode the same day it starts, instead of after it's already eaten a week of submissions.
