What this means

Sample sizes are tiny (2–4 active users/week) — read every number below as directional, not statistical.
Strongest signal

Core data-entry activity exploded week over week: contacts added (2→16) and sources submitted (2→17) both grew ~7–8x. The handful of active users are going much deeper into the core workflow once they're in.

Biggest concern

None of that growth reached the AI query step. Onboarding conversion from source capture to first AI query collapsed from 50% (last week) to 0% (this week), and total AI queries stayed flat at 5 even as upstream volume grew 7x.

  1. 1. Close the gap between "Source Captured" and "First AI Query." Funnel conversion on this step fell from 50% → 0% while contacts/sources grew 7x with AI Query Run volume flat at 5/5. Add an in-product nudge ("Ask Almura about this person") that fires immediately after source_submitted completes, so the first AI query happens in the same session instead of becoming a separate step users skip.
  2. 2. Fix active-user/retention instrumentation before trusting it. $identify — the event used for DAU/WAU/retention — logged zero events on Jun 16–18 despite heavy person_added/source_submitted/profile_viewed activity those same days, and shows 0% week-1 retention for the only eligible cohort (3 users, week of Jun 7). That 0% is more likely a missing-event artifact than real churn. Fire $identify (or a dedicated session_started event) on every app open, not just first login, so active-user and retention numbers reflect real usage.
Data gaps flagged, not skipped: no event for "relationship nudge surfaced/acted on" exists in the schema — nudge engagement cannot be measured until it's instrumented. New-signup counts are derived from $identify and inherit the same under-counting risk noted above (3 new in wk of Jun 7, 1 new in wk of Jun 14 — directional only).
