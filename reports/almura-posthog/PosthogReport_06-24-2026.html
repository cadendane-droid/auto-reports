How to read this

These metrics intentionally are not additive across event types. Keep these pairs separate:
  • person_added (new contact added) ≠ source_submitted (a source was captured) — one capture can add multiple people, or zero.
  • graph_opened ≠ $pageview on /network — pageviews include reloads/navigation noise; graph_opened is the real engagement signal.
  • profile_viewed ≠ $pageview on /people/[id] — same reasoning, profile_viewed reflects intentional viewing.
"Active users" above counts unique people who did source_submitted OR query_asked — not raw identify/pageview activity. With ~10 total users in the system, all percentages and rates here are directional, not statistically significant.
